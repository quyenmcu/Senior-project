# Senior-project
Driver fatigue is a significant factor contributing to road accidents, leading to severe injuries and fatalities worldwide. While traditional usage-based insurance (UBI) models assess risk based on driving behavior metrics such as speed, braking, and acceleration, they often fail to incorporate real-time fatigue detection, which is a critical element of driver safety. This research proposes an AI-driven UBI system that integrates computer vision and deep learning to detect driver fatigue in real time and incorporate this information into dynamic insurance premium calculations. The proposed system employs convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and telematics data to assess driver drowsiness and risky driving behaviors, enabling a more accurate and fair evaluation of individual risk levels. Additionally, a deep learning-based predictive model is utilized to adjust insurance premiums dynamically, ensuring that safer drivers receive lower premiums while high-risk drivers face appropriate penalties. By bridging the gap between fatigue detection and risk-based insurance pricing, this study aims to improve road safety, encourage responsible driving behavior, and enhance the fairness of UBI models. The results of this research could provide insurance companies with a more comprehensive framework for real-time risk assessment, ultimately leading to safer roads and more equitable insurance pricing.
