The driving analysis section provides a comprehensive breakdown of your driving habits and patterns, offering valuable insights to help improve road safety. By analyzing behaviors such as sudden braking, over-speeding, lane drifting, and fatigue-related actions, the system identifies potential risks that could lead to accidents. This detailed feedback empowers users to recognize areas for improvement and take proactive steps toward safer driving.

The analysis is powered by advanced machine learning algorithms, ensuring precise and personalized results. Drivers can track their progress over time and make informed decisions to refine their skills. For example, consistent feedback on frequent speeding or harsh braking allows users to adjust their habits, leading to a smoother and safer driving experience.

This section also integrates seamlessly with other features, such as the UBI (Usage-Based Insurance) system, enabling drivers to see how their habits impact their premiums. With an easy-to-use interface and actionable recommendations, the driving analysis section becomes an essential tool for cultivating responsible driving habits and ensuring safety for all road users.
